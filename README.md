01 — THE PROJECT

WELCOME TO THE GRID.

In the year 2099, the classic Snake experience has been reimagined.

CYBER // SNAKE is a futuristic arcade game combining neon visuals, responsive controls, dynamic challenges and an immersive cyberpunk atmosphere.

Your mission is simple:

SURVIVE. ADAPT. EVOLVE.

05 — HOW TO PLAY

LEARN THE CONTROLS.

CONTROL	ACTION
↑ ↓ ← →	Move
W A S D	Alternative movement
SPACE	Activate Turbo Boost
P / ESC	Pause the game

YOUR OBJECTIVE

1. Collect energy cores to increase your score.
2. Avoid obstacles and collisions.
3. Use power-ups strategically.
4. Beat your previous records.
